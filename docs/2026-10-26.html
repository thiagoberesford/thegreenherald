[Nepal] Nepal secures $21.6 million from UN climate loss and damage fund after deadly August floods
First payout from the UN’s Loss and Damage Fund aims to rebuild flood-hit regions and strengthen resilience.
Nepal will receive $21.6 million from the UN’s Loss and Damage Fund to address the devastation caused by catastrophic floods in August, which killed hundreds and displaced thousands. The funds will support reconstruction, early warning systems, and community resilience programs in the hardest-hit areas. UN officials called the payout a critical step in operationalizing the fund, which was established at COP27 to compensate vulnerable nations for climate-induced disasters. "This is a lifeline for communities struggling to recover," said a UN spokesperson. The funds will be disbursed through a partnership with the Nepal government and local NGOs.
