[Nepal] UN Loss and Damage Fund provisionally approves $21.6 million for flood-hit Nepal
First major payout from the fund aims to address urgent recovery needs after devastating monsoon floods.
The UN Loss and Damage Fund has provisionally approved $21.6 million to support Nepal’s recovery from catastrophic monsoon floods that displaced thousands and destroyed critical infrastructure. The funding, announced by the UN Office for Project Services, will prioritize housing, healthcare, and livelihood restoration in the hardest-hit regions. Nepal’s climate envoy highlighted the approval as a critical step toward operationalizing the fund, which was established at COP27 to compensate vulnerable nations for climate-induced losses. "This is a lifeline for communities struggling to rebuild," the envoy said. The payout follows Nepal’s appeal for immediate financial aid to address the disaster’s aftermath.
