Climate adaptation could create or protect 150m jobs: World Bank
New report finds adaptation investments could unlock economic gains while shielding workers from climate shocks.
Investing in climate adaptation could generate or safeguard up to 150 million jobs globally by 2030, according to a World Bank analysis released today. The report highlights sectors like agriculture, construction, and renewable energy as primary drivers of job creation. The study underscores the dual benefits of adaptation—boosting resilience while driving economic growth. World Bank officials emphasize that without urgent action, climate-related disruptions could erase existing employment gains in vulnerable regions.
