[Brazil] Brazil publishes first global ‘roadmap’ for phasing out fossil fuels
Landmark plan outlines 2040 phase-out targets and just transition frameworks ahead of COP31.
Brazil has unveiled the world’s first comprehensive roadmap to eliminate fossil fuels, setting 2040 as the target for ending coal, oil, and gas use across its economy. The plan prioritizes renewable energy expansion, green hydrogen development, and social safeguards for workers in traditional energy sectors. Environment Minister Marina Silva called the roadmap a 'paradigm shift' that aligns with Brazil’s COP30 commitments, while analysts note its potential to influence global climate negotiations. The document includes sector-specific timelines and funding mechanisms to support the transition.
