Emerging economies leapfrog the West in solar adoption
Solar power is surging ahead in Africa, Asia and Latin America, outpacing Europe and the US in clean energy transition speed.
A new analysis by DW.com reveals that emerging economies are embracing solar power and skipping fossil fuels at a pace far exceeding Europe and the United States. The shift is driven by falling costs, decentralized energy needs and urgent climate pressures, with countries like Kenya, Vietnam and Brazil installing solar capacity at record rates. Experts attribute the acceleration to the modular nature of solar technology, which allows rapid deployment without costly grid infrastructure. "These economies are not just catching up—they are rewriting the playbook for energy transition," said an energy analyst quoted in the report.

BeZero Carbon flags premiums for high-rated carbon credits
Analysis finds stronger demand and price premiums for credits with robust verification and permanence.
A new report from BeZero Carbon indicates clear evidence of price premiums for higher-rated carbon credits, reflecting growing market confidence in verified and long-lasting offsets. The findings underscore the importance of transparency in carbon markets as companies seek credible pathways to net-zero.
