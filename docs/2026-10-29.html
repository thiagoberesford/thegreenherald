[India] India seeks $220 billion clean energy push to meet 2030 target
Government unveils sweeping plan to triple renewable capacity and slash coal dependence by decade's end.
India’s government has outlined a $220 billion investment plan to triple its renewable energy capacity to 500 gigawatts by 2030, aiming to cut coal’s share in power generation from 70% to 30%. The proposal includes $150 billion for solar, wind, and storage projects, with the remainder directed toward grid upgrades and transmission infrastructure. Prime Minister Narendra Modi framed the push as both an economic opportunity and a climate imperative, stating that the transition would create millions of jobs and reduce air pollution. Analysts warn that financing gaps and land acquisition hurdles could slow progress, but the plan signals a decisive shift away from fossil fuels.
