[United Kingdom] UK electric car registrations surge 54% to capture third of market
New registrations hit record high as battery-powered vehicles outpace petrol and diesel for the first time.
Electric car registrations in the UK jumped 54% in October, pushing their market share to a record 33.4%, according to official data. The surge marks the first month battery-powered vehicles outsold petrol and diesel cars combined. Industry analysts attribute the milestone to expanded model availability, stronger charging infrastructure, and tightening emissions regulations. The shift aligns with the UK’s 2030 ban on new petrol and diesel car sales.
