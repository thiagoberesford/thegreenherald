UN nature summit approves two key agreements after marathon negotiations
Historic pacts on biodiversity finance and species protection adopted after tense final hours in Montreal.
Delegates at the UN Nature Summit in Montreal approved two landmark agreements late Wednesday, capping days of grueling negotiations. The first establishes a $100 billion annual fund to support biodiversity conservation in developing nations, while the second commits signatories to halt and reverse biodiversity loss by 2030. Environmental groups cautiously welcomed the outcomes but warned that implementation would hinge on political will and adequate financing. "These agreements are a step forward, but the real test begins now," said a spokesperson for the World Wildlife Fund.
