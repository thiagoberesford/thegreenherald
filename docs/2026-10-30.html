[China] China’s clean tech exports slash global emissions, report finds
Record-breaking solar, wind and battery shipments cut CO₂ by 1.2 gigatonnes in 2025 alone.
A new analysis reveals that China’s surge in clean technology exports—solar panels, wind turbines and electric-vehicle batteries—prevented the equivalent of 1.2 gigatonnes of CO₂ emissions globally last year. The report attributes the drop to displaced fossil-fuel generation and accelerated renewable adoption worldwide. Researchers note that without China’s clean tech surge, the world would have emitted an additional 3% of annual energy-related CO₂ in 2025. The findings underscore the central role of Chinese manufacturing in meeting global climate goals, even as domestic demand for renewables continues to rise.
